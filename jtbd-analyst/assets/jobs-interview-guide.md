# Jobs Interview — Discussion Guide

A 1–2 page prompt list for the **interviewer** (not a survey for the participant). Recruit actual job performers. Run with two researchers (interviewer + observer). 60–90 min. Let it flow; use this to get back on track.

## 0. Open (set expectations) — 3 min
- Thank them; explain it's about *how they work*, not your product.
- No right/wrong answers; recording consent.

## 1. Background & the job — 10 min
- Tell me about yourself and what you do.
- When was the last time you did [main job]?
- Walk me through that most recent time, start to finish.
- How did it feel overall?

## 2. Main & related jobs — 10 min
- What are you ultimately trying to accomplish?
- What problems are you trying to prevent or resolve?
- What else are you trying to get done around the same time?

## 3. Process — 20 min  *(sketch it together)*
- How do you get started? What's the very first thing?
- What's the next step? And after that?
- How do you make decisions along the way?
- How do you know you're doing it right?
- How do you wrap things up / know it's done?

## 4. Needs — 15 min
- What workarounds have you built?
- What do you dread or avoid? Why?
- What's the most annoying part? Why is that frustrating?
- What could be easier or faster? What would "good enough" look like?

## 5. Circumstances — 10 min
- In which situations do you do this differently?
- What conditions change your decisions?
- How does the setting / timing affect how you feel?

## Critical-incident probe (use when answers go generic)
1. Recall a specific time this went badly.
2. What happened, what went wrong, how did you feel?
3. What *should* have happened — what would have been ideal?

## Close — 3 min
- Anything I didn't ask that I should have?
- Thank them; explain next steps. (Hold any product Qs until now.)

**Interviewer reminders:** listen > talk; ask "why?" to zoom out, "how?" to zoom in; never ask them to design solutions.
